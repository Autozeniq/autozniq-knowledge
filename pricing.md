# AutoZeniq Pricing

## Overview

AutoZeniq operates on a subscription-based software-as-a-service (SaaS) model. Platform pricing is structured around resource allocations, including support agent seats, connected communication channels, knowledge base files, and automated message credits.

To view current plan listings, price tiers, and active promotional discounts, refer to our official **[Pricing Page](https://autozeniq.com/pricing)**.

---

## Subscription Parameters

Subscription levels determine platform capacities across four main areas:

*   **Agent Seats**: The maximum number of support staff members who can simultaneously log in to access the Unified Inbox.
*   **AI Message Credits**: The monthly quota for automated replies processed and generated by the AI Agent.
*   **Connected Channels**: The limit of active social media pages (Facebook Messenger, Instagram DM) and WhatsApp Business API phone numbers connected to the tenant dashboard.
*   **RAG Document Quota**: The total storage volume and file upload limit allocated for indexing RAG knowledge documents.

---

## Billing and Payment Methods

*   **Billing Cycles**: Available in monthly or discounted annual intervals.
*   **Bangladeshi Taka (BDT) Support**: Optimized for regional businesses, pricing is billed in BDT.
*   **Local Payment Options**: Subscriptions are paid securely online, supporting gateways like:
    *   **bKash**
    *   **Nagad**
    *   **Visa / Mastercard** (Local and international debit/credit cards)

## FAQ

### Q: What happens if I consume all my AI message credits before the month ends?
**A:** When credit limits are reached, the AI Agent is temporarily silenced. Customer messages will continue to arrive in the Unified Inbox under the `unassigned` queue, allowing human agents to reply manually. Additional credits can be purchased, or the plan upgraded, to re-enable automation.

### Q: Does manual replying by human agents consume message credits?
**A:** No. Only replies generated and sent automatically by the AI Agent consume message credits.
