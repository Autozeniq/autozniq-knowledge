## Core Product Offerings

AutoZeniq operates through three core divisions:

1.  **AI Agents**: Conversational reasoning engines utilizing Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to automate customer support and lead capture.
2.  **Customer Support Automation**: Consolidated multi-agent workspace (Unified Inbox) managing conversations across WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, Instagram, and web chat widgets.
3.  **Commerce Automation**: Storefront database synchronizations (WooCommerce & Shopify) to automate order tracking, invoice payment links, and dispatch routing.
