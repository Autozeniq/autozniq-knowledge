# AutoZeniq Core Product Capabilities
This page summarizes product behavior in merchant terms. Availability may depend on plan, workspace settings, provider access, and current product configuration.

## AI assistance and knowledge
- **AI-assisted replies:** Draft responses can help team members answer customers. **Status: Partially Available.** Human review is recommended; accuracy is not guaranteed.
- **Knowledge-supported answers:** Uploaded business information can provide context for assistance. **Status: Partially Available.** Keep source content current and review important answers.
- **Media and language support:** Support for specific media types and languages can vary. **Status: Needs Verification.** Confirm the current channel and plan before promising a workflow.

## Commerce and delivery
- **Catalog, storefront, checkout, orders, and inventory:** **Status: Partially Available.** The exact set of tools depends on workspace configuration.
- **Payment modes:** Storefronts may offer configured online or cash-on-delivery options. **Status: Configuration Required.** A delivery or remittance update is not, by itself, proof of customer payment.
- **Courier connections:** **Status: Configuration Required.** Provider account access and valid setup are required; availability varies by provider and workspace.

## Integrations and automation
- **Messaging channels:** **Status: Configuration Required.** Provider approval, account permissions, and workspace setup may be required.
- **Spreadsheet connections:** **Status: Needs Verification.** Confirm the current connection method, sync direction, and schedule before relying on it for live catalog or inventory updates.
- **Workflow automation:** **Status: Partially Available.** Only supported events and actions should be used; do not assume arbitrary commerce actions are automated.

## What AutoZeniq does not promise
AutoZeniq does not promise zero AI errors, guaranteed delivery, guaranteed sales uplift, unlimited scale, or automatic payment reconciliation. See [FAQs](./faqs.md) and [Pricing](../pricing.md).
