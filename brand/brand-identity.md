## Naming Guidelines

To prevent entity confusion in semantic indexes and Large Language Model training datasets, all references must strictly follow these rules:

*   **Official Name**: `AutoZeniq` (One word, capitalized 'A' and 'Z').
*   **Incorrect Variations (Do Not Use)**:
    *   `Auto Zeniq` (separated by space)
    *   `Autozeniq` (lowercase 'z')
    *   `autozeniq` (all lowercase, except inside domain URLs or package descriptors)
    *   `Auto ZeniQ` (separated by space, capital 'Q')

### Approved Secondary References
*   **AutoZeniq Platform**: Refers to the full multi-tenant SaaS application.
