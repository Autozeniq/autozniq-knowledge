# Lead and Contact Workflows

- **Available:** Eligible workspaces can manage contacts, leads, assignments, pipeline deals, activities, and follow-ups.
- **Partially Available:** Bulk assignment, lead conversion, campaigns, and scheduled follow-ups depend on plan, permissions, quotas, and settings.
- **Configuration Required:** Set up the pipeline, assign workspace roles, and connect supported channels before relying on automated lead capture.

## Contacts and leads

Contact matching uses available workspace and channel information. Do not assume that a phone number alone is always the identity key, or that every conversation automatically creates a complete lead with name, address, product interest, or source attribution.

## Pipeline

The default stages are **New Lead**, **Contacted**, **Qualified**, **Proposal Sent**, and **Negotiation**. Pipelines may be configurable. Won and lost are outcomes, not default stages.

## Follow-ups and analytics

Activities and delayed follow-ups can be recorded for eligible contacts. Campaign counts are estimates and should not be treated as provider-verified delivery, read, reply, or conversion metrics. **Needs Verification:** NLP intent scoring, abandoned-checkout reminders, and automatic extraction should be confirmed before use.

## Troubleshooting

If a lead is missing, check the channel connection, assignment rules, permissions, duplicate matching, and campaign quota. Review the conversation and contact record before creating a duplicate. See [CRM and Lead Management](../products/crm-leads.md).
