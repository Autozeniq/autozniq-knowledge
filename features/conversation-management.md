# Conversation Management and Shared Inbox

- **Available:** Eligible workspaces can search conversations, view message history, send human replies, and assign conversations.
- **Partially Available:** AI pause, takeover, resume, selected real-time updates, and text/image/voice handling depend on channel and workspace setup.
- **Configuration Required:** A supported provider connection, channel permissions, workspace role, and applicable plan entitlement.

## AI and human handling

AI Copilot suggestions are drafts for agent review. Automated replies, where enabled, require additional configuration and are not guaranteed for every channel. Pause or take over AI handling when a conversation needs human judgment.

## Limits

Do not assume that every conversation displays order history, customer scoring, ticket fields, internal notes, typing indicators, presence, read receipts, collision locks, or automatic team routing. Do not promise universal duplicate-reply prevention or a sales uplift.

## Troubleshooting

If a conversation is missing, confirm the provider is connected, the account has permission, the webhook or message sync is healthy, and the conversation is within the workspace scope. If replies fail, verify the channel is still approved and the message type is supported. Refresh after reconnecting a provider and contact support with the conversation time and channel.
