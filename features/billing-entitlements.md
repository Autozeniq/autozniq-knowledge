# Billing and Entitlements

**Status: Partially Available.** This page helps merchants understand their workspace plan, usage, limits, invoices, and which tools may be enabled. An **entitlement** is a feature-access rule linked to a workspace’s plan and configuration. What a workspace can use also depends on its role, account, and provider setup. See [Pricing](../pricing.md) for the current public catalog.

## Plans, trial, and setup flow

The published catalog lists Starter, Growth, and Enterprise. Prices, included limits, trial terms, and availability can change; use the current Pricing page rather than an older plan table. Enterprise terms are arranged with sales.

To check a plan change, review the current plan and usage in the workspace, consult the current Pricing page, and use only the checkout and payment option enabled for the account. A change is complete only after the billing screen confirms it and the payment is verified. A quote or pending payment is not an activated upgrade. Keep invoice and payment confirmations for support.

## Subscription payments

- **Configuration Required:** Subscription checkout and payment verification depend on a provider being enabled for the workspace.
- **Configuration Required:** Provider access, correct currency and amount, and billing permissions.
- **Partially Available:** Plan changes, scheduled downgrades, manual payments, and add-ons may depend on account-specific configuration.
- **Needs Verification:** A payment method shown on a screen is not proof that it is enabled for your account.

Payment methods, upgrades, renewals, refunds, invoices, and live availability depend on the account and configured provider. Do not treat a displayed option or payment request as completed until the workspace confirms it and the payment is verified.

## Storefront payments

Storefronts may offer cash on delivery, advance plus cash on delivery, full online payment, or partial payment when configured. These options can be set independently by the workspace. Payment status should reflect a verified provider payment or verified collection; courier remittance alone is not proof of customer payment.

## Affiliate program

**Not Available:** The public affiliate program is closed. Do not rely on commission, approval, or payout availability.

## Troubleshooting and related features

If a feature is unavailable, check the plan, usage allowance, workspace role, and provider status. For a failed payment, confirm currency, amount, billing details, and provider verification, then retry only when the billing screen indicates it is safe. Contact support with the invoice or payment reference; never send secret credentials.

The public catalog does not establish account-specific payment or feature availability.
