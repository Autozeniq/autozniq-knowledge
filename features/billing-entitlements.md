# Billing and Entitlements

**Status: Partially Available.** Plan, usage, invoice, and entitlement details depend on the workspace billing experience, account, and provider setup. What a workspace can use depends on its plan, usage, role, and configuration. See [Pricing](../pricing.md) for the current public catalog.

## Plans and trial

The published catalog lists Starter, Growth, and Enterprise. Prices, included limits, trial terms, and availability can change; use the current Pricing page rather than an older plan table. Enterprise terms are arranged with sales.

## Subscription payments

- **Configuration Required:** Subscription checkout and payment verification depend on a provider being enabled for the workspace.
- **Configuration Required:** Payment provider access, correct currency and amount, and billing permissions.
- **Partially Available:** Plan changes, scheduled downgrades, manual payments, and add-ons may depend on account-specific configuration.
- **Needs Verification:** A payment method shown in a screen is not proof that it is enabled for your account.

A plan change is complete only after the billing screen confirms it and the payment is verified. A quote or pending payment is not an activated upgrade. Keep invoice and payment confirmations for support.

## Storefront payments

Storefronts may offer cash on delivery, advance plus cash on delivery, full online payment, or partial payment when configured. Payment status should reflect a verified provider payment or verified collection; courier remittance alone is not proof of customer payment.

## Affiliate program

**Not Available:** The public affiliate program is closed. Do not rely on commission, approval, or payout availability.

## Troubleshooting

If a feature is unavailable, check the plan, usage allowance, workspace role, and provider status. For a failed payment, confirm currency, amount, billing details, and provider verification, then retry only when the billing screen indicates it is safe. Contact support with the invoice or payment reference; never send secret credentials.
