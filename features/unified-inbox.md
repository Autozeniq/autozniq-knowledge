# AutoZeniq Unified Inbox

**Status: Partially Available.** The Unified Inbox helps merchant teams find, review, reply to, and assign customer conversations in one workspace. It is for support staff and other permitted team members who handle customer questions. Which channels and tools are available depends on provider approval, workspace setup, role, plan, quota, and deployment.

## What you can do

- **Available:** Eligible workspaces can list and search conversations, view history, send human replies, and assign work.
- **Partially Available:** Open, Pending, and Resolved filters and mobile drawer behavior may vary by workspace and deployment.
- **Partially Available:** Channel connections, selected real-time updates, media handling, and AI pause or take-over controls vary by provider and channel. Do not assume a particular named channel or message type is enabled.
- **Configuration Required:** Provider authorization, channel permissions, workspace role, plan access, and available quota.

## Basic workflow

Connect and approve a supported channel, then send a test message and confirm that it appears in the workspace. Search or filter the conversation, review its history, and reply or assign it to a team member. AI Copilot suggestions are drafts for human review. If available for that channel, pause AI handling before a person takes over, then resume it only when appropriate.

## Limits and troubleshooting

Automated replies require additional settings and are not guaranteed on every channel. Do not promise automatic assignment based on agent load, collision locks, typing or presence indicators, read receipts, or internal notes. Shopify or WooCommerce purchase history, complete ticketing, arbitrary workflow actions, and provider template messages need confirmation for the specific workspace and channel.

If a channel is unavailable, confirm provider approval, account permissions, workspace access, and channel status. If history or replies are missing, check that the connection is active and send a new test message. If the issue remains, use the current support route and include the channel, approximate time, and displayed error; never share credentials.
