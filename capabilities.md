# AutoZeniq capabilities

AutoZeniq brings customer support and selected commerce workflows into a merchant workspace. Availability can vary by plan, permissions, provider account, and configuration. For the complete feature-by-feature guide, see [Features and Availability](./docs/current-feature-inventory.md).

## Support and AI

- **Shared inbox — Partially Available:** channel-specific actions and media support vary; see [Unified Inbox](./features/unified-inbox.md).
- **AI assistance — Partially Available:** AI can assist with replies and other support tasks; review important customer-facing answers before sending. Automatic replies need separate confirmation and setup; see [AI Assistance](./products/ai-agent.md).
- **Business knowledge — Configuration Required:** add accurate, supported information and confirm that it has processed before relying on it in responses; see [Knowledge Sources](./features/knowledge-base.md).
- **Workflow automation — Partially Available:** only use triggers and actions shown as enabled in the current workspace; see [Workflow Automation](./features/automation.md).

## Store, products, orders, and delivery

- **Storefront — Configuration Required:** set up content, catalog, checkout, and domain before inviting customers; see [Storefront Builder](./products/store-builder.md).
- **Products, inventory, and orders — Partially Available:** supported actions depend on workspace settings and plan; see [Order Management](./products/order-management.md).
- **Courier connections — Configuration Required or Needs Verification:** confirm each provider independently before relying on bookings, tracking, returns, or settlement; see [Delivery and Logistics](./products/delivery-logistics.md).
- **Payment records — Partially Available:** record a payment only after the applicable method confirms it. Courier delivery, cash-collection instructions, and remittance are not proof of customer payment.

## Business and integrations

- **CRM and leads — Needs Verification:** confirm the workflows and plan availability for your workspace; see [CRM and Leads](./products/crm-leads.md).
- **Google Sheets — Partially Available:** confirm access, mapping, and a successful sync; see [Google Sheets](./integrations/google-sheets.md).
- **Subscription plans — Available as published offers:** see [Pricing](./pricing.md) for current prices and public plan details.
- **API, SDKs, webhooks, and security commitments — Needs Verification:** confirm the current supported contract and account scope before implementing; see [Developer Integrations](./integrations/api.md) and [Security and Privacy](./security.md).

**Planned** is reserved for capabilities announced on an official public roadmap. A status of **Needs Verification** is not a roadmap promise.
