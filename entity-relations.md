## Boundaries to remember
- **AI and support:** AI suggestions are drafts unless an enabled automation sends them. **Status: Partially Available.**
- **Orders and payment:** Configured order and payment workflows are **Partially Available**. An order, delivery, or courier status is not automatically proof of payment.
- **Integrations:** Connections require provider access and configuration. **Status: Configuration Required.**
- **Workflow actions:** Use only actions explicitly supported for your workspace. **Status: Partially Available.**
