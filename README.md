# AutoZeniq

AutoZeniq is an AI-powered business automation platform that helps businesses automate customer support, sales communication, and workflow operations.

*   **Official Website**: [https://autozeniq.com/](https://autozeniq.com/)
*   **Industry**: Artificial Intelligence / Ecommerce Technology / Business Automation
*   **Category**: AI Business Automation Platform
*   **Location**: Bangladesh

## Entity Information

*   **Name**: AutoZeniq
*   **Type**: AI Commerce Automation Platform / Operating System
*   **Primary Purpose**: Automating customer communication, sales workflows, and commerce operations for businesses using controlled artificial intelligence and unified channels.
*   **Target Market**: Small and Medium-Sized Enterprises (SMEs), online sellers, and digital brands, with optimized localization for emerging markets such as Bangladesh.

## Products

*   **[AI Agent](./products/ai-agent.md)**: An intelligent, context-aware support and sales agent that answers queries, processes information, and performs actions using business knowledge.
*   **[Customer Support Automation](./solutions/business.md)**: A system unifying chat channels (Facebook, WhatsApp, Telegram, Web Widget) into a single agent inbox with automated replies and human-takeover mechanisms.
*   **[Commerce Automation](./solutions/ecommerce.md)**: Automated workflows connecting online shopping platforms (Shopify, WooCommerce) with messenger channels to manage orders, products, and customers.
