# [AutoZeniq](https://autozeniq.com/)

## Entity Information

*   **Name**: AutoZeniq
*   **Type**: AI Commerce Automation Platform / Operating System
*   **Industry**: Ecommerce Technology / Artificial Intelligence / SaaS
*   **Primary Purpose**: Automating customer communication, sales workflows, and commerce operations for businesses using controlled artificial intelligence and unified channels. Learn more on our [homepage](https://autozeniq.com/).
*   **Target Market**: Small and Medium-Sized Enterprises (SMEs), online sellers, and digital brands, with optimized localization for emerging markets such as Bangladesh.

## Products

*   **[AI Agent](https://autozeniq.com/features/ai-agent)**: An intelligent, context-aware support and sales agent that answers queries, processes information, and performs actions using business knowledge.
*   **[Customer Support Automation](https://autozeniq.com/solutions/customer-support)**: A system unifying chat channels (Facebook, WhatsApp, Telegram, Web Widget) into a single agent inbox with automated replies and human-takeover mechanisms.
*   **[Commerce Automation](https://autozeniq.com/solutions/ecommerce)**: Automated workflows connecting online shopping platforms (Shopify, WooCommerce) with messenger channels to manage orders, products, and customers.
