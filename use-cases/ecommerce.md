# AutoZeniq for E-commerce Businesses
AutoZeniq may help an online business manage products, customer conversations, storefront activity, and orders. **Status: Partially Available.** Confirm the required tools and connections before launch.

## Example workflow
1. Keep product and policy information available to the support team. **Status: Partially Available.**
2. Review customer messages and use AI to draft a response. **Status: Partially Available.** Review before sending.
3. Manage catalog and order information in the available commerce tools. **Status: Needs Verification.**
4. Connect a delivery provider where supported. **Status: Configuration Required.**
5. Confirm payment through the applicable payment process. **Status: Configuration Required.** A courier status is not payment proof.

## Important limits
Do not promise two-way spreadsheet sync, automatic dispatch for every order, error-free inventory, instant checkout from every social conversation, or guaranteed conversion results. Confirm the current channel, provider, plan, and workspace configuration.
