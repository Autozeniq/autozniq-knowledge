# AutoZeniq for Online Sellers

Online sellers may use AutoZeniq's shared inbox and AI assistance to handle customer conversations, maintain product and policy knowledge, and coordinate orders. The reviewed codebase also includes a storefront, catalog/inventory features, CRM tools, and courier adapter paths. Actual channel and provider availability depends on account permissions, configuration, and deployment.

## Supported workflow examples

1. A customer messages a connected channel; an agent can view conversation history and ask the AI Copilot for a draft reply. A suggestion is not automatically sent unless a separately enabled AI auto-reply path applies.
2. An agent can manage catalog-backed order information through the workspace's supported order flow. Product photos or voice notes may be processed on supported media paths; no universal product-match accuracy or cost saving is promised.
3. A seller can manage orders and make a courier booking through an available provider integration after credentials and provider account setup are complete.
4. A seller can configure storefront payments and track payment stages. COD collection credit remains disabled until there is verified customer-collection evidence; a courier status or remittance record is not payment proof.

## Limits

Do not promise public-comment-to-private-message automation, instant checkout from every social conversation, automatic dispatch for every confirmed order, customer SMS/WhatsApp notifications, a fixed weekly time saving, or error-free inventory sync without confirming the exact provider, feature settings, and deployment.
