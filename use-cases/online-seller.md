# AutoZeniq for Online Sellers
Online sellers may use AutoZeniq to organize customer messages, product information, orders, and team follow-up. **Status: Partially Available.** Channel and provider access must be confirmed.

## Example uses
- Review conversations in a shared workspace and assign follow-up. **Status: Partially Available.**
- Ask AI for a draft reply based on available business information. **Status: Partially Available.** Review before sending.
- Manage catalog and order information where the workspace supports it. **Status: Needs Verification.**
- Connect a delivery provider after account setup. **Status: Configuration Required.**

A courier update or remittance record does not prove customer payment. Do not promise public-comment automation, instant checkout from every conversation, automatic dispatch for every order, universal media matching, or fixed time savings.
