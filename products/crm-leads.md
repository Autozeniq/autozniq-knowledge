# AutoZeniq CRM and Lead Management

**Status: Needs Verification.** CRM and Leads can help merchants organise customer and lead information, assign work, review sales stages, and record activities or follow-ups where enabled. It is intended for eligible shop owners and team members who handle customer conversations and sales opportunities. Confirm which tools are available for your workspace, role, plan, and connected channels before relying on them.

## Basic workflow and setup

Where available, review a contact or lead, check its related conversation or order context, assign it to the right team member, and record an activity or follow-up. Pipeline stages and deal actions depend on workspace configuration. Setup may require an eligible CRM plan or capacity, suitable permissions, connected channels where relevant, and configured stages or activities. Live availability must be checked in the workspace.

## Contacts, leads, and campaigns

- **Needs Verification:** Contact and lead lists may provide search, organisation, assignment, sales-stage, and related conversation or order context. Confirm available fields and actions in your workspace.
- **Needs Verification:** Bulk assignment, lead conversion, campaign broadcasts, and automatic capture are not confirmed as available for every workspace. Check the current controls before building a process around them.
- **Needs Verification:** Do not assume that every conversation creates a complete lead or captures every customer field. Duplicate matching depends on the information available in the workspace and channel.

Where enabled, activities and follow-ups can be recorded for eligible contacts. Campaign counts are not provider-verified delivery, read, reply, or conversion metrics. Intent scoring, abandoned-checkout reminders, and channel-source attribution are **Needs Verification**.

**Not Available:** The public affiliate program is closed; commission and payout availability should not be promised.

## Limits, troubleshooting, and related features

If a record is missing, check role, plan, channel connection, available quota, assignment, and the information used for duplicate matching. Review the contact and conversation before creating a new record.
