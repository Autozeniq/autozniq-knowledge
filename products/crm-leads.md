# AutoZeniq CRM and Lead Management

**Status: Needs Verification.** CRM tools may help eligible teams manage contacts, leads, deals, activities, follow-ups, and campaigns. Confirm which actions are enabled for your workspace, role, plan, and connected channels before relying on them.

## Contacts and leads

- **Needs Verification:** Contact and lead lists may provide search, organization, assignment, lifecycle, and related conversation or order context. Confirm the available fields and actions in your workspace.
- **Partially Available:** Lead conversion, bulk assignment, campaign broadcasts, and automatic capture depend on permissions, quotas, and channel setup.
- **Needs Verification:** Do not assume that every conversation automatically creates a complete lead or extracts every customer field.

Contact matching uses the information available from the workspace and channel; a phone number alone is not guaranteed to be the universal deduplication key.

## Deals and pipeline

Pipeline stages and deal actions depend on the workspace configuration. Confirm the current options before setting up team processes around them.

## Follow-ups and campaigns

Where enabled, activities and follow-ups can be recorded for eligible contacts. Campaign counts are not provider-verified delivery, read, reply, or conversion metrics. Intent scoring, abandoned-checkout reminders, and channel-source attribution are **Needs Verification**.

## Affiliate program

**Not Available:** The public affiliate program is closed; commission and payout availability should not be promised.

## Troubleshooting

Check role, plan, channel connection, campaign quota, assignment, and duplicate matching when a record is missing. Review the contact and conversation before creating a new record.
