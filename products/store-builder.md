# AutoZeniq Storefront Builder

**Status: Configuration Required.** This guide helps merchants check which storefront and checkout options are enabled in their workspace before inviting customers to an online shop. Availability can vary by workspace and connected services; confirm the options shown in your workspace.

## Merchant setup flow

1. Review the product information, stock, and customer-facing policies you intend to show.
2. Check which page-editing, theme, and checkout controls are enabled for your workspace and plan.
3. Configure the payment and delivery options you intend to offer, if those services are enabled for your account.
4. Preview the customer-facing pages and test the available checkout flow before publishing or sharing the shop.
5. For a custom domain, verify ownership and DNS, then wait for the current TLS and domain checks to succeed. Do not promise instant activation.

Products, inventory, provider settings, tax or policy content, and customer or shipping settings may be prerequisites for the options shown. **Partially Available:** Themes and page options vary by workspace and plan. Confirm the current choices in your workspace; do not assume that every shop has the same pages or checkout methods.

## Domains

**Configuration Required:** Managed subdomains and custom domains depend on current workspace setup and domain readiness. Custom domains require ownership verification, DNS configuration, TLS readiness, and a current successful check. **Needs Verification:** Activation time and certificate handling vary by deployment; do not promise a fixed or instant activation time.

## Tracking and privacy

Configured store tracking can distinguish analytics, advertising, and matching purposes. Optional purposes start denied, and provider delivery depends on valid consent and provider configuration. A dashboard acknowledgement is not proof that a provider processed an event.

## Troubleshooting and related features

If a page is not public, confirm it was published and that the correct domain is being used. For a custom domain, check DNS, ownership, TLS, and verification freshness. If checkout fails, confirm that the relevant product, stock, payment, and delivery settings are enabled; check any coupon setting only if it is shown as available. Preview again before publishing changes.
