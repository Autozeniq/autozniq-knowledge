# AutoZeniq Storefront Builder

**Status: Configuration Required.** Storefront editing and publishing depend on workspace access and setup. Confirm that the specific pages, checkout options, and domain are ready before inviting customers.

## Store and checkout

- **Available:** Product and category pages, cart, checkout, order tracking, coupons, configured payment modes, and delivery quotes where the related commerce features are enabled.
- **Configuration Required:** Products, inventory, payment provider, delivery provider, tax or policy content, and customer/shipping settings must be configured before launch.
- **Partially Available:** Themes and page options vary by workspace and plan. Current default theme choices should be confirmed in the workspace.

## Domains

- **Configuration Required:** Managed subdomains and custom domains require current workspace setup and domain readiness.
- **Configuration Required:** Custom domains require ownership verification, DNS configuration, TLS readiness, and a current successful check.
- **Needs Verification:** Activation time and automatic certificate handling vary by deployment; do not promise instant activation.

## Tracking and privacy

Configured store tracking can distinguish analytics, advertising, and matching purposes. Optional purposes start denied, and provider delivery depends on valid consent and provider configuration. A dashboard acknowledgement is not proof that a provider processed an event.

## Troubleshooting

If a page is not public, confirm it was published and that the correct domain is being used. For a custom domain, check DNS, ownership, TLS, and verification freshness. If checkout fails, verify product publication, stock, payment, delivery, and coupon settings. Recheck preview before publishing changes.
