# AutoZeniq AI Assistance

**Status: Partially Available.** AutoZeniq can help eligible support teams draft responses, retrieve information from connected knowledge sources, and handle supported text, image, or voice inputs. Exact availability depends on plan, workspace role, AI settings, model/provider access, and channel connection.

## What is available

- **Available:** AI Copilot drafts for human review.
- **Partially Available:** Knowledge retrieval that can use processed workspace sources.
- **Partially Available:** Automated replies and media-assisted workflows on supported channels.
- **Configuration Required:** Current knowledge sources, eligible AI settings, provider approval, and a successful test conversation.

Agents can pause, take over, or resume AI handling where the workspace supports those controls. AI output is not a guarantee of factual correctness, fixed latency, cost savings, conversion, or support deflection. Verify policies, prices, stock, and order details before acting.

## Troubleshooting

If AI does not respond, check entitlement, channel connection, model/provider status, AI settings, and knowledge-source processing. If a draft is incorrect, update the relevant source and keep human review enabled. Do not share credentials in prompts or support requests.
