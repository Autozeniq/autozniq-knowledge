# Workspace Roles and Access

**Status: Partially Available.** Workspace owners and authorized administrators can manage team access where the feature and plan allow it. Available roles, permissions, and seat limits vary by workspace.

## Roles and permissions

Use the roles shown in your workspace. The workspace owner and administrator roles have different access; **Needs Verification:** confirm the exact permissions for each role before granting access. Give each teammate only the access needed for their work.

## Invitations and access changes

**Configuration Required.** The inviter needs permission, the workspace must have a seat available where applicable, and the invitee must use the correct email address. Invitation acceptance and activation are **Needs Verification**; check the current invite status rather than assuming the teammate is active. Remove access promptly when someone leaves. Never share an owner account or secret credentials.

## Troubleshooting

If an invitation is missing, check the email address, spam folder, seat allowance, and whether the invite has expired. If a teammate cannot see a feature, check their role, plan entitlement, workspace selection, and provider-specific permissions. Ask the workspace owner or support to correct access; do not request privileged platform administration.
